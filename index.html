FranchiseFlowPartner selection & onboarding engine
AI x Ops Hackathon · Case 1

Every franchise applicant decided, explained and onboarded in seconds.

Drop in the Applications + Territories Excel. FranchiseFlow checks every application, gives out territory slots without ever putting two stores in one pin code, and hands each partner their next steps, onboarding plan and a message in their own language.

No login Runs in your browser Same file, same answer Sample file

How it works

1Check

Reads messy forms (“35 lakhs”, “seven”, “600 sqft”) and flags missing or contradictory answers.

2Score

Fit out of 100 on funds, space, experience, timeline and territory, with every point shown.

3Allocate

Best partner first, one store per pin, never next to our own stores, open slots respected.

4Onboard

Next steps, week-by-week plan, training in Kannada, Hindi or English, and a ready message.

What makes it different

Path to yes

For anyone not approved, it tests every lever and shows the smallest change that gets them in.

Talk to it

“Why is APP-007 on hold?” or “APP-012 found a ground-floor space”: every decision re-runs live.

Speaks their language

Drafts each partner’s decision message in Kannada, Hindi or English.

Human in the loop

Close calls are flagged for review, managers can override with a reason, and everything is logged.
